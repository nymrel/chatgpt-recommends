Questions, answered plainly

Before you ask

Does this tool talk to ChatGPT for me?

No. You run the prompts yourself — that's the whole point. You see exactly what a real customer sees when they ask an AI assistant who to hire. The tool only writes the questions and scores the text you paste back. It never queries ChatGPT, and it never pretends to.

Is my data safe?

Yes. Your inputs and pasted answers are processed in this browser and saved only in its local storage until you reset them. The hosted page loads aggregate Vercel Insights analytics, but your form values and pasted answers are not included in those requests.

How does the score work?

For each answer you paste, the scorer checks whether your business is named: not mentioned scores 0, a plain mention scores 60, leading the answer scores 100, and a mention wrapped in negative context (words like "avoid" or "complaints" near your name) scores 15. Your overall score is the average of the answers you actually paste — never an estimate.

Which AI assistants can I use?

Any of them. The prompts are plain questions, so ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, and Gemini all work. Paste whichever answer you get — the scorer reads the text the same way regardless of which assistant produced it.

What do I get in the $9 Full Report?

All 8 buyer-intent prompts written out ready to copy, a breakdown of every answer you paste back, and the fix checklist — llms.txt, FAQ schema, an AI-bot robots allowlist, and entity proof — as a print-ready report. It also includes a calendar reminder dated 30 days out and a saved baseline file, so the recheck is a real before-and-after. You still run the prompts yourself; the tool never queries an AI for you.
